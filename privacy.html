Privacy Policy

What we collect when you visit this site, why we collect it, and how you stay in control of it.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (like your name, email, and phone) so we can answer you and schedule calls.
  • We never sell your information.
  • We never share your mobile number or text message consent with anyone for their marketing.
  • You can stop text messages any time by replying STOP.
  • Please do not send personal health information through this website.

1. Who we are

This website, workinghealthyu.com (the "Site"), is run by Accelerate Therapy and Performance ("Accelerate," "we," "us," or "our"). We provide onsite injury prevention programs for employers. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through the Site, our online forms, our chat and scheduling tools, and our email and text message communications.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you schedule a call, use our Cost Savings Calculator, chat with us, sign up for emails, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name, job title, and company name
  • Email address and phone number (including mobile number)
  • Business details you choose to share, such as workforce size, industry, location, and injury or claims figures you enter into the calculator
  • Anything you write in a message or chat

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site and the tools on it may automatically collect:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages you view, links you click, how you arrived at the Site, and time spent on pages
  • Whether you opened or clicked an email we sent

We use this to keep the Site running, understand what content is useful, and improve our marketing.

Cookies and similar tools

Cookies are small files your browser stores. We and our service providers use them to remember preferences, keep the chat and scheduling tools working, and measure Site traffic. They do not store your name or contact details on their own. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, like chat or scheduling, may not work properly if you do.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and schedule calls
  • To send you the reports, resources, and information you request
  • To send emails and, if you agree, text messages about our services, appointments, and resources
  • To understand how the Site is used and make it better
  • To protect the Site and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To meet legal obligations

4. Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we may text you about scheduled calls, follow-ups, and information related to our injury prevention services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of doing business with us.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. This excludes only the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf, who may not use it for anything else.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us run our business, such as our website host, our customer relationship and scheduling platform, chat and email tools, and website analytics and SEO tools. They may only use your information to perform services for us.
  • Business changes. If Accelerate is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that deal.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Accelerate, our clients, or others.

6. Health information

This Site is built for employers and business contacts. Please do not submit personal health information, medical records, or details about a specific employee's injury through our forms, chat, or calculator.

Health information we handle while providing care or services is protected under applicable law, including HIPAA where it applies, and is governed by our separate Notice of Privacy Practices and our agreements with client employers, not by this website policy. Patients of our physical therapy clinics can find our clinic privacy notices at accelerate-pt.com.

7. How we protect your information

The Site uses SSL encryption, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we collect. No website or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, keep reasonable business records, and meet legal requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Emails: Click "unsubscribe" in any marketing email.
  • Texts: Reply STOP to any message.
  • Cookies: Adjust your browser settings.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights. We will not treat you differently for using them.

10. Children

The Site is intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other websites

The Site may link to other websites, such as our job listings or social media pages. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
