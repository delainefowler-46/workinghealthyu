The financial case

Your injury program is an insurance cost strategy.
Most employers don't treat it that way.

Every aspect of our program is designed to move the financial levers that drive your workers' compensation premiums — measurably and permanently.

Loss frequency

Fewer claims hit your loss runs — and underwriters notice

Our onsite specialists catch ergonomic fatigue and physical stress through real-time coaching and early intervention before it ever becomes a reportable incident. Fewer claims on your loss runs signal a controlled safety culture to carriers — expanding your marketplace options and lowering your baseline premium rates.

60%average reduction in recordable incidents in Year 1
E-Mod protection

Keep injuries out of the indemnity column — where they cost the most

When a minor strain occurs, our specialist provides immediate first-aid level care within OSHA guidelines — keeping the incident classified as Medical-Only. Many states apply a 70% discount to Medical-Only claims in the E-Mod calculation. Keeping an injury out of the indemnity column prevents your modifier from spiking, directly saving you in future premium penalties.

70%discount applied to Medical-Only claims in most state E-Mod formulas
Reporting lag time

The longer a claim waits, the more it costs — we eliminate the wait

In a typical facility, an employee might push through ergonomic fatigue for two weeks until a minor issue becomes a serious one. With a specialist on your floor, employees have zero-barrier access the moment they feel physical stress. That lag time drops from weeks to minutes — and claims reported within 24–48 hours cost up to 50% less than delayed ones.

50%lower average claim cost when reported within 24–48 hours
Return-to-work

Every day an injured employee sits home, your claim grows

When an injury does require off-site care, our specialists act as the bridge between the physician's restrictions and your floor — designing and monitoring modified duty roles that get employees back working safely. Instead of collecting indemnity checks at home, they're on light duty. The carrier stops paying wage replacement, the claim stops growing, and your Experience Period data is protected.

3:1minimum ROI on prevention program investment
