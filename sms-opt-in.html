Quick answers

What kind of texts will I get?

Reminders about calls you schedule with us, follow-ups after those calls, and, only if you check the second box, occasional updates about our injury prevention services.

How often will you text me?

Message frequency varies. Most people get a few texts around a scheduled call. Marketing texts, if you opt in, are typically no more than a few per month.

How do I stop the texts?

Reply STOP to any message. You will get one text confirming you are unsubscribed, and then the texts stop. Reply HELP for help.

Do I have to agree to texts to work with you?

No. Text consent is never required to schedule a call or buy any service.
